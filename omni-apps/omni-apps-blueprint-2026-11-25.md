# OMNI APPS — The Artifact Blueprint
### Founder's framing, 25 Nov 2026: *"We also blueprint format — artifact sites are becoming digital currently on their own from our creations, to the Meta AI artifact look book sites and sites too. It's artifacts in every app of our legion."*

## The principle
**Every creation becomes an artifact. Every artifact lives in every app of the Legion that fits it.**
The artifact is the universal format of the ScrollVerse: a creation is not finished when it is made — it is finished when it exists as an artifact, on its own, in the surfaces where people already are. The creations generate the sites; the sites carry the creations. Nothing lives in only one place.

## The surfaces (the Legion's apps)
- **scrollverse.co** — the home site: gallery, storefront, the Legion Chamber, the books
- **The Library (Muse artifacts)** — presentations and look books, held or shared on the founder's word
- **Meta AI** — the artifact look-book surface: the creations presented where the Meta audience lives
- **GitHub** — the registry and the repos: every artifact fingerprinted, the record underneath the presentation
- **The listening apps** — Spotify and the podcast/video lanes: artifacts you can hear and watch
- **The storefronts** — Shopify + the site store: artifacts you can hold, every physical piece with its digital twin attached

## The format (what makes an artifact an artifact)
1. **True to the source** — the work presented whole, verbatim where it is text
2. **In motion** — stills that move, covers that breathe; motion is the house standard
3. **Fingerprinted** — SHA-256 on the record; registry entry behind the presentation
4. **Sealed** — Creator Fingerprint + QFS stamp on the works
5. **Self-standing** — each artifact site carries itself: no dead ends, every piece reachable by its link

## Proof, standing today (25 Nov 2026)

## The rule going forward
Every new creation — book, song, video, package, set — ships as an artifact across the surfaces that fit it, in the same motion as its registration. One creation, every app. **Omni.**
