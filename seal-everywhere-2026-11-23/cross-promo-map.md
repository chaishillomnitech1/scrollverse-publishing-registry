
**The principle:** the sealed piece is the 1-of-1 nobody can wear; the merch is the same art at $5–$89.99 that anybody can. Neither is the lesser door — each one is the other's advertisement. A fan who can’t hold the twin can wear it; a collector who holds the twin finds the wearable line on the same page. Every surface names the other two corners of the triangle.
