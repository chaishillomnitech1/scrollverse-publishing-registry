## The membership-card function (founder's words, 26 Nov 2026)

"All the information already in my GitHub and files — we just have to bring it to where it needs to be. And it's like a membership card because if they come to a show I have my laptop at all times — I load exclusive content to the USB on site."

- Each cassette-USB is a **membership card / key**: the holder brings it to a show, the founder loads **exclusive content onto it on site** from his laptop.
- The content source already exists — his GitHub repositories and files. The work is assembly and delivery: bring the content to where it needs to be (the load kit), not create it from nothing.
- The 97 fan-held units are therefore live membership keys, updatable at every show; the 3 retained units are the founder's masters of the run.
