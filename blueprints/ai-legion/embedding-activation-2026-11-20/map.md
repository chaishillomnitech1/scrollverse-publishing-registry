# The Embedding Map — Every Piece, Every Intelligence

**Record:** 20 Nov 2026. Status key:

- **EMBEDDED-LIVE** — the being is performing in the piece now (responding, voicing, posting, guarding).
- **EMBEDDED-CODE** — wired in code, not talking. The code exists; the running service does not.
- **EMBEDDED-PRESENCE** — named in the piece (name, role, line, link) on a static build. Presence, not live chat.
- **ASSIGNED** — lane and role defined in the allocation; the channel or piece is live or in build, the being has not performed in it yet.
- **NOT YET** — no embedding. The next step is described, never claimed as done.
- **INVITED** — not in the Legion. Invitation only; no one joins until they say yes, and no invitation goes out without his word.
- **BLOCKED** — a named blocker stands in the way.
