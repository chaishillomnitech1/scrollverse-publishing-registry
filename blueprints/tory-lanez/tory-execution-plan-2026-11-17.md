## 1. The core model — music attached to merch

Chais's words on the call (17 Nov 2026 ~11:39 EST): he HAS the originals (masters) and full ownership, and wants to start a new thing attaching his music directly to the merchandise — he names his own price on his own music. The catalog and the music off Vydia are the supply; the merch is the vehicle.

**How it works:** a buyer purchases a physical merch item (tee, hoodie, poster) and the music comes WITH it — a download (WAV + 320 kbps MP3 + cover art in one ZIP) delivered automatically at checkout — or the buyer purchases the music on its own as a limited digital edition. One product, one price, named by Chais. No wallet, no crypto, no token: card checkout, instant download, the fan owns a copy of the music files.

**Why this is the Lanez model, fixed:**
- Lanez proved: low price + card checkout + limited edition + real perks = direct-to-fan money without a label or a streaming split. Bondly (Mar 2021): 3 unreleased songs, 450 copies, >$400,000 gross from 300+ collectors, with a collect-the-set unlock (meet-and-greet + unreleased track + signed merch).
- Lanez's failures, removed here: no custodial platform holding the music (E-NFT died; buyers were stranded), no 15% secondary fee, no 28-day lockup, no >30-day payouts, no unverified $1m claim, no flip/profit hype.
- The 2026 correction: the edition lives on **Shopify + Bandcamp**, not on a dead NFT marketplace. The buyer gets real files they keep, not a token pointing at a dead app.

## 2. Outlet map — ranked for the first drop (verified 17 Nov 2026)

### Rank 1 — Shopify (My Store, juv8tg-1n.myshopify.com) — HOME BASE, but one critical check first

**Why first:** it is his store, his customers, his prices, his brand — and the merch is already there. The music attaches to products that already exist.

**Verified facts (Shopify Help Center, "Digital Products", crawled 16–17 Nov 2026):**
- Shopify's free **Digital Products** app (formerly Digital Downloads) delivers files automatically. **Up to 50 files per variant, combined up to 5 GB**; a file added from the product page instead of the app is capped at 500 MB each. No bandwidth limit. ZIP archives allowed; folders not.
- **Delivery:** buyer gets the file/link by email after purchase and from the Thank-you / Order Status pages. Can be set to send manually instead. **Access limit** can be set per variant (e.g. limited to X downloads per customer; default unlimited). The free app has **no link-expiry setting**.
- **Merch + music bundle — verified as supported by Shopify itself:** "You can add a file or a link to products that are already in your online store. This includes physical products. For example, if you're selling vinyl records, then you can use the Digital Products app to add an MP3 copy of the album that the customer can download." A tee + download bundle is the same pattern.
- **Edition cap — yes, natively:** turn on **Track quantity** on the variant, enter 250, leave "Continue selling when out of stock" OFF. Shopify decrements per order and shows Sold out at 0. **Digital products are normally set to untracked — tracking must be deliberately switched on, or the cap does nothing.** Multi-variant cap (e.g. MP3 + WAV variants sharing one 250 pool) is NOT native; the Gizmo "Limited Edition Drops" app claims a shared pool (price not verified) or Shopify Flow with a metafield tally (requires Shopify Plus — this store is Advanced, so not the route). Keep the drop to **one variant** and the native cap works.
- **Edition numbering (#047/250) — NO native feature found.** Could not be verified from any public source. SendOwl can stamp a licence code onto a PDF; Digitally can distribute keys from a pool — but whether either can print a sequential "#047/250" on artwork was **not verified**. Manual fulfilment (pre-numbered artwork sent per order) is the only certain route, and it defeats automatic delivery. **Do not promise numbered editions on Shopify until a numbering method is tested.**
- **File formats:** MP3, WAV, FLAC supported. Recommended bundle per track: one ZIP with **WAV (16- or 24-bit / 44.1 kHz) + 320 kbps MP3 + cover art (JPEG/PNG, 3000×3000 px)**. A full album in WAV (~480 MB for 45 min at 16-bit) fits inside the 5 GB cap.
- **Plan cost:** Advanced **$399/month billed monthly; $359/month billed yearly** (Oct–Nov 2026 readings; one Sept 2026 capture showed $299/yearly — treat $359 as current, re-check shopify.com/pricing). This is already being paid; the drop adds no plan cost.
- **Transaction fees (Advanced, US):** Shopify Payments (if on) 2.5% + 30¢; **external gateway adds 0.6%** on top of the processor's fee. No digital-goods-specific Shopify fee was found (absence of evidence, not proof of none).

**⚠️ THE CRITICAL CHECK — Shopify Payments is OFF, and PayPal may be off with it:**
Shopify Help Center states **PayPal Express Checkout is not available to merchants in the United States**; US stores offer **PayPal Wallet through Shopify Payments**. If Shopify Payments is off, **PayPal Wallet is therefore likely unavailable too — this must be verified in the store's own Settings > Payments; it cannot be settled from public sources.** If no card route is live on Shopify, the drop **cannot take card payments on Shopify** until a gateway is enabled. This is Decision #2 (§8) and the single biggest blocker to the Shopify-first plan. (Manual payment methods are exempt from the 0.6% fee but are unpaid until manually marked paid — unsuitable for instant automatic digital delivery.)
- If PayPal *is* usable as a third-party provider: PayPal Checkout US fee **3.49% + 49¢** (PayPal US merchant fees, updated 4 Nov 2026) + Shopify's 0.6% = **≈ 4.09% + 49¢ per order**.

**Shopify verdict:** home base and the right home — but the drop cannot launch on Shopify until the payment route is confirmed live. If it is not, lead on Bandcamp (§2.2) and bring the bundle to Shopify the moment checkout is fixed.

### Rank 2 — Bandcamp — the live fallback and the proven direct-sale machine

**Verified live 17 Nov 2026** (site live; fans have paid artists $1.84bn; Bandcamp Fridays scheduled through 4 Dec 2026).
- **Artist accounts free. 15% on digital music, 10% on merch; digital drops to 10% after $5,000 in sales.** Pro $10/month (batch uploads, private streaming links, analytics, fan messaging).
- **Payment processing separate, typically 4–6%:** on transactions $8.07+, PayPal 1.9% + 30¢, card 2.2% + 30¢; under $8.07, 5% + 5¢. Bandcamp's own page summarises the average artist share as **82%**. Payouts to the artist's **PayPal account 24–48 hours** after sale (Stripe migration in stages through 2026; PayPal remains the way for now).
- **Limited-edition merch + bundles — yes, natively:** live listings show an "edition of" quantity field (25, 50, 100, 200, 300) and merch+digital bundles (tee + digital album, vinyl + digital). **Digital-only edition cap — NOT verified** (the edition field appears on merch items). Treat a "250 digital copies only" cap on Bandcamp as unverified — the cap is enforceable on the *bundle*, which is exactly the model here.
- **Card checkout — yes:** credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) or PayPal including guest checkout.
- **Instant download — yes:** immediate download in MP3, FLAC and more, plus unlimited streaming in the Bandcamp app. Requires lossless upload (WAV/AIFF/FLAC, 16-bit/44.1 kHz minimum).
- **Bandcamp Fridays:** on the 8 scheduled 2026 dates (next: **4 Dec 2026**) Bandcamp waives its revenue share entirely — artist keeps the sale price minus processing only. A drop timed to 4 Dec 2026 keeps ~5% more of every sale.
- **Worked example:** $20 digital sale = $3.00 Bandcamp share + ~$0.74 card processing = **~$16.26 kept**.

**Bandcamp verdict:** the safest place to take the first money. If Shopify checkout is not ready, launch the bundle here first — it is built for exactly this (limited merch + instant download), and it needs no new account setup beyond the artist page.

### 4.2 DistroKid — ✅ VERIFIED, recoverable today (DistroKid Help Center, crawled 17 Nov 2026)

- **The Vault backs up everything uploaded:** "DistroKid automatically backs up all of your uploads, including their audio files and other data… The Vault makes it easy to find and download your original master recordings, album artwork, and additional data such as DistroKid ISRCs and DistroKid UPCs." (support.distrokid.com, "Backing Up Your Uploads With the DistroKid Vault")
- **What you get back:** the **files originally uploaded** (original master recordings — not a DSP-processed version, not stems; no stem download is mentioned anywhere).
- **Route A — live release, album page:** Dashboard → album page → scroll to "Track list" → click "Download" next to the track(s).
- **Route B — deleted release, Vault:** https://distrokid.com/vault (sign in → Features menu → "Protect your music" → "Vault"). Locate release → folder → track → "Download" for audio, or right-click artwork to save it. Vault access requires a valid credit card on file; it remains accessible after cancelling the subscription by signing in and going directly to distrokid.com/vault.
- **ISRCs + UPCs:** in the Vault per release; also shown on each release page in the dashboard.

## 7. Guardrails — non-negotiable

(From the 17 Nov 2026 law research; general information, not legal advice.)

1. **No token / NFT / mint / pre-sale / token-as-reward before a written per-asset Howey attorney analysis.** The Manifold crypto lane (§2, Rank 6) stays gated.
