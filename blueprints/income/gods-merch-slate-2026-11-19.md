## Art rule (kept)

ONLY our own generated art. Tees use the full generated image; posters use
the film still (frame at 5s) from the episode's clip. Never feed
screenshots, never another creator's book cover or ad.

**Images are NOT attached to the drafts yet** — the Shopify connector takes
public HTTPS image URLs only; local files are not supported, and expiring
hosted links would break. Art attaches at the Printify POD step (Printify
upload), which is also where real size variants and the print provider are
set. Each product below lists its exact art file.

**Variants today:** one Default Title variant per product (price set,
inventory untracked, quantity 0). Real tee sizes and poster sizes come from
Printify at the POD step.
