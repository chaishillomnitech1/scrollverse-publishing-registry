## Legal gate checklist — per asset

The same checklist applies to each of the six collectibles. An asset
advances only when every item is checked for that asset. Clearance is
per asset, not per series.

| # | Gate item | Status (all six) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Written per-asset Howey analysis, signed by a licensed attorney, answering the 12 questions in the Howey brief (`~/workspace/legal/howey-brief-wave1-2026-11-18.md`) as adapted to this asset | NOT requested — NOT received |
| 2 | Attorney's IP assessment recorded for the asset (own generated art; no third-party flag — to be confirmed in writing) | NOT requested — NOT received |
| 3 | Chain and platform chosen by the founder | NOT chosen |
| 4 | Edition size and price chosen by the founder with the attorney | NOT chosen |
| 5 | ROSEVAULT per-asset release record — ALL five items: (a) source file or authoritative URL, (b) rights evidence, (c) contributor verification, (d) destination URI, (e) explicit owner release record | INCOMPLETE — items (d) and (e) missing for every asset; (a) exists (art paths above); (b) and (c) are the founder's own generated art, to be evidenced in the release record |
| 6 | Founder sign-off on that specific asset — its name, description, art, edition, and launch — after items 1–5 clear | NOT given |

**Rule:** if any item is missing, the asset stays in staging / local-only.
Do not work around the gate. No mint, no sale, no pre-sale, no listing,
no contract deploy, no token creation until all six items check for that
asset.

**Note on CF-1 (governance §11.5):** the ROSEVAULT gates and the standing
autonomous permission conflict and are unresolved. This slate follows
ROSEVAULT: staging only, no release, until the founder decides otherwise
in a recorded decision.

## Head-start rule

No collectible in this slate is given, airdropped, reserved, or promised
as a reward for buying merch, and no collectible is attached to any bundle
or head start, until Gate 1 (item 1 above) has cleared for that asset and
the founder has approved the head start for that asset. Until then,
bundle incentives are Stage 1 only (loyalty credits, early access to the
launch announcement, numbered runs, Library access, earned roles), as
defined in the Early Coins governance §5.
