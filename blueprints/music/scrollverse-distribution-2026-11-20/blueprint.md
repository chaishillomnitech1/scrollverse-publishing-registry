# ScrollVerse Distribution — Blueprint

Built 20 Nov 2026. Founder: Chais Hill. Status: BUILT — nothing sent, nothing sold, nothing spent.

## The name

**ScrollVerse Distribution.**

Rationale: it carries the ScrollVerse name artists already know from Chais's world, and the word "Distribution" says exactly what it does in one breath. It is deliberately distinct from "ScrollVerse Records" — Records is the imprint for Chais's own catalog under his self-record deal; Distribution is a service for other artists. No artist will confuse buying a service with signing to a label, because the name makes the split plain.

## The model (one paragraph)

ScrollVerse Distribution puts independent artists' music out through Chais Hill's Vydia dashboard for a flat fee. The artist keeps 100% of their rights, 100% ownership of their masters, and 100% of their publishing — ScrollVerse never takes a royalty share of the artist's music unless the artist themself chooses the optional Partner tier. Splits are set by the artist per release and stay the artist's. ScrollVerse places the music where the artist can't reach on their own — 250+ platforms including JPay and every DSP, Smart Links, Content ID, promo across Chais's network — and every release drives activity and traffic back to ScrollVerse. The artist stays in control. ScrollVerse gets paid and gets traffic. Nobody signs their music away.

## The pricing model — by budget, pass-through

Packages are priced **according to budget**, because a struggling artist can start small and a bigger budget buys more. The model works like this:

- **The artist pays for the Vydia paid options they want** (Vevo, Content ID upgrades, Dolby Atmos, extra stores — whatever Vydia charges for). Those are **pass-through at Vydia's list price** — ScrollVerse adds no markup to Vydia's own price.
- **ScrollVerse adds a fair service fee — "our tax" — for applying the tools and running the release.** That is the service fee in each tier, plus a small per-option application fee where a paid option is added.
- **Everyone happy:** the artist keeps their rights and gets the reach, ScrollVerse gets paid for the service. **Money flow (his words): they pay us, we pay them — the artist pays the package fee up front; Vydia's royalties for their music come to ScrollVerse as the account holder, and ScrollVerse pays the artist their royalties in full, per the splits the artist set. The fee is never taken out of the artist's royalties** (except the optional Partner tier, which the artist chooses).

Full tiers: `packages.md`.

## What the artist keeps control of

- Masters: 100% theirs, always. ScrollVerse holds a delivery copy only, for distribution.
- Publishing: 100% theirs. ScrollVerse never takes publishing, never registers itself as publisher on an artist's work.
- Splits: set by the artist per release (writer, producer, features) through Vydia payees. ScrollVerse records what the artist says — it never invents a split.
- Takedowns: the artist can pull a release at any time. One message, no fee, no fight.
- Price and timing: the artist picks the release date (subject to the 5–7 business-day DSP review window, same as Chais's own singles).
- Their own accounts: the artist's Spotify for Artists, Apple Music for Artists, and socials stay theirs. ScrollVerse never asks for their passwords.

## The traffic flywheel

Every artist release is traffic into the ScrollVerse world:

1. The artist's fans hit the Smart Link → they land on ScrollVerse pages.
2. The release posts carry the ScrollVerse name, logo, and links → new eyes on Chais's catalog, books, Vault, and sites.
3. The artist's catalog grows inside the ScrollVerse dashboard → the dashboard's activity rises, which is the story told to the next artist.
4. Artists who come in for distribution meet the whole world — the books, the Legion, the Vault — and some stay for that too.

"We don't chase, we attract." The distribution company is the attraction engine: artists come for the reach, and the reach grows because artists come.

## Onboarding flow

1. **Artist applies** — email or DM with: artist name, release title, WAV masters (44.1kHz/16-bit), cover art (square 1,400–3,200px), songwriter/producer credits, split sheet (who gets what, in writing), PRO (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC/none), and a rights confirmation ("I own or control this recording and I have the right to distribute it").
2. **ScrollVerse checks** — masters play clean, cover meets the square rule, credits complete, splits add to 100%, rights confirmation on file. Anything missing goes back to the artist once, in one message.
3. **Delivery** — ScrollVerse delivers the release through the Vydia dashboard (Composition + release, same spec as Chais's own 15 singles), sets the artist's payees and splits in Vydia, generates the ISRC, attaches the Smart Link.
4. **Release** — artist picks the date (minimum 7 days out for DSP review). Promo push goes out across the network on release day.
5. **Royalties** — they pay us, we pay them: Vydia pays ScrollVerse as the account holder, and ScrollVerse pays the artist their royalties in full, per the splits the artist set. ScrollVerse's fee is the flat fee, paid up front — never taken out of the artist's royalties.
6. **Reporting** — monthly, from Vydia's own numbers: streams, earnings, where it played. Verified numbers only.
