## THE PHYSICAL — made on demand, never on a shelf
*Available as an add-on to any package, or standing alone for an existing catalog.*

Physical used to mean pressing a thousand copies and praying. Ours is manufactured **when a fan orders it** — demand creates the supply, so there is no inventory bill, no warehouse, no dead stock, ever.

- **USB** — the signature format: full album + videos + storybook files on a branded USB (the ScrollVerse cassette-USB line, first run 2024). The collector's object that also carries the digital world with it
- **Vinyl** — 12" records manufactured on demand, one copy or a numbered limited edition (edition caps enforced: when the run sells out, it's sold out)
- **CD** — retail-quality discs in full packaging, manufactured per order in ~24 hours and drop-shipped to the fan
- **Merch** — print-on-demand apparel and goods through the store's existing Printify/Shopify rails: printed when ordered, fulfilled from print partners near the buyer, worldwide
- **Accessories** — the collector's ecosystem around the music: record sleeves and display frames, USB cases and presentation boxes, apparel and goods accessories — sourced from the physical-option companies that already make them, offered alongside every format
- **Bundles** — the ScrollVerse specialty: vinyl + USB + storybook + merch in one collector package, each piece made on demand, assembled by the order
