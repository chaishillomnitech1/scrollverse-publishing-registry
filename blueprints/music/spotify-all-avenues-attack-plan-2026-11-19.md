## §1 — WHAT THE ALGORITHM ACTUALLY REWARDS (2026)
- **Save rate is the strongest signal.** A track with 10K streams and an 8% save rate beats 100K streams with a 0.3% save rate. Raw stream count is LOW weight.
- **30-second rule:** streams under 30 seconds count for nothing — no royalty, no algorithm weight. Skip rate is the most damaging metric.
- **The 28-day window:** the first 28 days after a release are the highest-leverage days a track ever has. Day 0–7: Release Radar push + editorial decision. Day 14–28: Discover Weekly evaluation — strong signals get you into the recommendation loop.
- **Saves, repeat listens, streams per listener, playlist adds** — in that order. Passive playlist streams are discounted; active, intentional listening is what counts.
- **Release cadence:** artists releasing a single every 4–6 weeks grow 40–60% faster in monthly listeners than sporadic releasers (Chartlex, 300+ campaigns).
- **Ads beat playlisting under $1,000** (Southworth, Dec 2025): an ads-promoted song reached 12,000 listeners and 3,200+ saves; a playlist-promoted song reached 24,000 listeners and only 155 saves. Put money where the save rate is.
