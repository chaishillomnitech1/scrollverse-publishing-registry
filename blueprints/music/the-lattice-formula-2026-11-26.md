## 2. THE FORMULA — STEP BY STEP

Every catalog, album, single, or art set that enters the system passes through the same eight steps, in order. The steps are the process; the process is the product.

### Step A — LOG + TRACK (registry intake)
The work is entered into the house record with its full identity: titles, writers, ISRCs, UPC, release IDs, and file inventory. Every file is fingerprinted at intake. Nothing enters the system anonymously — if it has no ID, it gets one; if it has no fingerprint, it is hashed before anything else happens to it.
*Worked example: the Goat album intake produced the verified 13-track record — titles, ISRCs, durations, UPC 764656068820, Release #2109376 — read back from the distributor's own pages, not assumed.*

### Step B — SEAL (Creator Fingerprint)
Each piece receives its registry signature in the form `SIG·<SET>-<PIECE>·<first 8 of SHA-256>`, computed over the actual file bytes. The seal line rides every certificate:

> 🌹 **ROSE GOLD SEAL** · QFS STAMPED · Creator Fingerprint · 963 Hz Crown · 528 Hz Guardian · DNA·CHAIS·AB6DA246ADE3F32C · Prophet Seal Twin-Prophet-01

The seal proves the contained work without revealing what is sealed inside it. Every encryption, fingerprint, and certificate in the system carries the QFS stamp alongside the Creator Fingerprint — nothing ships unstamped.

### Step C — GIT REGISTRY PUSH (public, timestamped, blob-verified)
The set's records — set record, seals, registries, certificates — are pushed to the ScrollVerse publishing registry on GitHub. A push is not claimed until it is proven: the new head commit is read back and every pushed file's blob SHA is matched against the local `git hash-object`. The registry is the public, timestamped proof layer: anyone can confirm a work was registered, and when, without the sealed contents ever being exposed.

### Step D — DISTRIBUTION RECORD (Vydia) WITH PRO IDENTITY RIDING THE DATA
The work is distributed through the Vydia lane (BRED Distro) to 64 destinations per delivered release. The artist's PRO identity travels **on the release data itself** — writer name and IPI, publisher name and IPI — so ASCAP registration rides the distribution automatically, at no additional cost and no separate portal process. This is the founder's doctrine, verified in practice: **the Vydia route makes ASCAP free.** Writer credits are audited across the whole catalog and corrected to the exact legal-artist form (see Proof Points: 110 of 110 tracks checked, 77 writer credits corrected and verified saved).
*Honest limit, on record: the distributor's dashboard exposes no publisher field in any flow; where a publisher attach requires it, the attach runs through the distributor's admin route (Zendesk request #48516, submitted 25 Nov 2026, open). The writer identity rides automatically; the publisher attach is pursued through admin until confirmed — and is never claimed as done before the read-back says so.*

### Step E — COPYRIGHT LINES ON EVERY RECORD
Every record the system produces — set record, certificate, registry entry, composition sheet — carries its copyright line (© holder + year, as the release itself carries it). The paid US Copyright Office eCO filing remains available per work; where it is not filed, the Lattice's timestamped registry chain (Steps A–D) stands as the dated, public proof of the work and its ownership. The formula never presents the registry as a government filing — it presents it as what it verifiably is.

### Step F — THE NEW ANGLES (added 26 Nov 2026)
Three layers no standard distributor runs:

1. **Composition seals.** Each composition is sealed independently of its recording: fingerprint = SHA-256 of the canonical string `TITLE|ISRC|WRITER|PUBLISHER|UPC`, reproducible by the creator alone, binding the written work to the writer and publisher IPIs. The Goat album's 13 compositions stand sealed as `SIG·COMP-GOAT-01…13`.
2. **QR signature layer.** Every piece, composition seal, and key receives a QR code whose payload states its registry signature and full SHA-256 fingerprint in plain text. Verification is offline and permanent: scan the code, hash the file, compare. Production spec: black on white, 4-module quiet zone, error-correction level Q (≈25% damage recovery). The Goat Set's 131 QR codes were **machine-decoded after generation and read back byte-for-byte against their intended payloads — 131 of 131, zero failures.**
3. **Digital Mirror Twin registry.** Where a physical run exists, every physical unit is bound, key for key, to a numbered digital twin in a twin registry — physical ↔ digital, one table, closed by a **Master Twin Fingerprint** (SHA-256 over the registry's canonical table) so any alteration to any row breaks the master hash. The Goat Set's twin registry binds all 100 keys; its Master Twin Fingerprint is `e34bb87934df29d4f920a11ff23c921cf1ad4a3b100d95397ffbf9e87319c62e`, independently recomputed from the printed table — exact match.

### Step G — EXCHANGES (every surface cross-linked)
Every surface attaches and cross-links every other: the registry entry points to the release; the release's Smart Link points to the set; the set's presentation points to the store, the registry, and the show; the physical piece's QR points back to its twin. Standing doctrine: *all routes open — exchange.* (Guard, absolute: family-held material is never publicly linked.) The exchange layer costs nothing to run and multiplies every other lane — a scan on a physical key can walk to the album, the seal, and the registry in three steps.

### Step H — PHYSICAL ANCHORS (where a physical run exists)
Where a release has a physical edition, the physical is the anchor and the digital is its twin — never the reverse. The standing model is the **cassette-USB membership key** (HP-016, the 2024 LogoTech run): 100 units in the world, each one a key its holder brings to a show, where exclusive content is loaded onto it on site from the founder's laptop. Registration is an upgrade, not a toll — an unregistered key still loads. Every physical or novelty sale in the system carries the full attached digital set as standard, not as an upsell: the NFT twin, the coin, the digital master, the full-resolution cover, and the registered composition sheet.

## 3. WHAT RUNS AUTOMATICALLY vs. WHAT RUNS ON THE CREATOR'S WORD

| Runs automatically through the process | Runs only on the creator's word |
|---|---|
| Registry intake — IDs, ISRCs, fingerprints (Step A) | Minting any NFT or coin set — the founder's start call, per platform, per set |
| Creator Fingerprint seal + QFS stamp on every piece (Step B) | Editions, prices, and royalty figures — set by the creator at the gate, never by the system |
| Git registry push with blob verification (Step C) | Any spend (filing fees, application fees, production orders) |
| Distribution delivery + PRO identity riding the release data (Step D) | Signing legal documents and attestations (agreements, W-9, ID verification) |
| Copyright lines on every record the system produces (Step E) | Proposing this formula — or any partnership — to any business |
| Composition seals, QR signatures, twin registry (Step F) | Public sharing of any presentation or set |
| Exchange cross-linking across surfaces (Step G) | Physical re-runs and new production orders |
| Show-load content delivery to key holders (Step H) | |

The split is the trust model: **the proof is automatic; the commerce is the creator's.** The system never mints, prices, lists, signs, or spends on its own motion.

## 5. WHO IT'S FOR — AND HOW IT ATTACHES

**Distributors** (CD Baby is the founder's named example): the Lattice is the proof-and-registration layer a distributor's catalog does not carry today — fingerprint seals, a public timestamped registry, composition seals, and QR verification on every release. A distributor adopting or partnering with the Lattice offers artists permanent, verifiable proof of ownership as part of distribution itself, instead of a receipt.

**Physical partners** (LogoTech is the founder's named example — the house's cassette-USB manufacturer since the 2024 HP-016 run): every physical unit that leaves the line can leave with its digital twin already bound — QR signature, twin-registry entry, and the attached digital set. The physical product stops being a souvenir and becomes a key: updatable at shows, verifiable by scan, traceable through the registry when it changes hands.

**Labels and independent catalogs:** the full chain — intake, seal, registry, distribution record, composition seals, twins — runs as a service over an existing catalog without taking ownership, a percentage of streams, or custody of masters.

**How it attaches to the packages:** the Lattice Formula is the engine inside the ScrollVerse Distribution packages (`~/workspace/music/scrollverse-distribution-packages-2026-11-25.md`). THE DOOR carries the single through Steps A–E plus its Single Set; THE SEAL adds the Album Set, per-track registry entries, and the seal certificate; THE FLOOD adds the World Set, video and storybook versions, and the full Step F–H treatment. **Package prices remain PROPOSED ($29 / $99 / $299) — the founder sets final prices; this formula changes none of them.** Terms as proposed there stand: the artist keeps 100% of store royalties and 100% of ownership; placement is permanent with no yearly fee.
