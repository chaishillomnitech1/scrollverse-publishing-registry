## 2. Launch sequence — the order, and why

The registry itself registers Wave 1 in launch order, SVRS first. The study (companion file) confirms the logic: the coins that win fair launches are the ones with the simplest story, the cleanest art, and a real audience already watching. The sequence below follows that — strongest and cleanest first, the flagged and the unfinished last.

**Order:**

1. **SVRS (ScrollVerse) — first.** The lead coin. Simplest story ("the coin of ScrollVerse"), his own IP with no flag, art already on file with a Pinata CID, and the @DuneSimpleSock profile already carries the emblem. It is the only coin where the only missing pieces are the three gates — no art gap, no IP flag, no extra decision. If one coin proves the machine, it is this one.
2. **GODS (The Gods) — second.** The community currency of a real show with real content behind it: six episodes written, six clips shot and verified, eight inspiration images in hand, all his own art. A coin attached to a teaching show has something a buyer can actually watch — that is the difference between a story and a slogan. Launch it when the first episode is public, so the coin and the show arrive together.
3. **Wave 4 — Music (SAVE, HIRF, CUNNIN, GOAT) — third.** Real songs with real audiences. The 15 Vydia singles land 27 Nov 2026; a coin per flagship song rides the music instead of asking the music to ride the coin. One coin per song, only after the song is out and the descriptions are written.
4. **Wave 2 — AI Legion (QUBIT, SOCRATICA, OMNI1, TATRAS, FLAME, SOLOMON, LYRIAN, PSYCHE16, QFS, AZURATH, NUR) — fourth.** Eleven coins, one per being. Strong names, real characters with real chats behind them — but art and descriptions do not exist yet for any of them. They launch in the order their art and descriptions are completed, not all at once. Eleven launches at once would split attention eleven ways; the study's case patterns all show one story at a time.
5. **Wave 3 — Films (6 coins) — fifth.** One coin per film, after the films are public and the titles/tickers are recorded. Same rule as the music: the work first, the coin after.
6. **Wave 5 — Books (4 coins) — sixth.** After the books are out (Book 05 is the only manuscript in hand; the rest are outlines). A book coin before the book exists is a promise with nothing behind it.
7. **The six Knicks coins (CYBORG, ANCHOR, GENERAL, WING, TOWER, ASSASSIN) — last, and only on a fresh explicit founder decision.** They carry a trademark and right-of-publicity flag the registry itself records, and the earlier as-is decision covered merch only, not a token. They do not move up the sequence, and they do not launch on a general "go" — each one needs its own explicit call from Chais after the flag is weighed.

**Why not all at once:** every case pattern in the study shows the same thing — a launch is a 72-hour attention event. Attention is the scarce resource, not the 0.02 SOL. Eight coins at once would split his own audience eight ways and give each coin a fraction of the launch window. One coin, one story, one window — then the next.
