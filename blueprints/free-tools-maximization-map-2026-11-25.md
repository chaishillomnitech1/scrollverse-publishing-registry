## THE COLLECTION LATTICE — every royalty door (founder, 25 Nov 2026)
*His words: "The mlc, songtrust, sound exchange, music reports, the harry fox agency."*
ASCAP is one door. The full lattice of collection, each body named for what it collects:
- **The MLC** (The Mechanical Licensing Collective) — US streaming mechanical royalties for songs, under the Music Modernization Act: the mechanicals every stream generates
- **SoundExchange** — digital performance royalties for the sound recordings themselves (non-interactive streaming, satellite, webcasting): paid to the recording owner and featured artist
- **The Harry Fox Agency (HFA)** — mechanical licensing: the licenses and mechanical accounting around reproductions of the compositions
- **Songtrust** — worldwide publishing administration (a commission-based administrator: an optional lever, weighed against his self-owned publishing posture — DESTINATIONHILL28 is his own house)
- **Music Reports** — the licensing/royalty administration the platforms themselves use: the other side of the counter, where licenses are cleared
**Posture:** every royalty stream has a named collector; the top-to-bottom push extends across the whole lattice. Each door is verified door by door before anything is claimed — a registration is a fact only when it reads back confirmed, and his compositions' current state (Vydia showed Publisher None / Composition Content EMPTY at audit) is exactly the gap this push exists to close.
