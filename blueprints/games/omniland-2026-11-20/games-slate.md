# OmniLand Games Slate — The Full Slate, Using His Tech

**Record:** 20 Nov 2026. **Build only — design, not a launch.** Nothing here is published, sold, rated, or submitted to any platform. Every game is original — his worlds, his stories, his music, his characters. No third-party IP anywhere: no Nintendo / Sony / Microsoft characters, worlds, or assets; chess is a public-domain game; all other titles are his own.

**The slate rule (from his own code):** every game works with the being off. A Legion being is a guide, never a dependency. Every game is browser-local first, no account, no wallet, no payment inside the game, no chat, no matchmaking, no data collection. Youth-facing play is adult-supported, per the Phase 584 / 658 boundaries.

**Guide assignments** follow the embedding map: Qubit guides first (captions, then voice after the narrator fork). Other beings join by lane only after the invitation queue and his word per game. Nothing below claims a being is embedded — all guide slots are QUEUED / NOT YET until he says so per game.

### W2-05 The Blueprint Games — Five Study Lanes, Playable

- **Genre:** Puzzle / study games. **Source:** Game Systems Research Blueprint (blob `2b3d242f…`), Phase 740 (blob `c2937b46…`).
- **The five games (one per lane):**
  1. **Systems mathematics** — puzzle economy, map generation, strategy literacy, transparent rulesets.
  2. **Physics + sciences** — experiment loops: observe, model, doubt, revise.
  3. **Philosophy + great minds** — debate, ethics, invention, historical-context quests. Not authority simulation.
  4. **Manuscript + text transmission** — archive puzzles: separate source, translation, attribution, interpretation, and original fiction.
  5. **Sound + frequency research** — sound sandbox: rhythm, pitch, spectrum, self-authored mood notes. **No therapeutic or medical claim, ever** — the file's boundary is the game's boundary.
- **Guide (queued):** Socratica (questions) + Qubit (captions); the heartbeat hook is the embedding point.
- **Music:** His tracks only in the sound sandbox lane.
- **Advertising angle:** "A game universe that studies itself." — the Blueprint's own line; five games for the ones who like the question more than the answer.
- **Status:** Design complete in the Blueprint. Needs: his word per lane, playable build per lane.
