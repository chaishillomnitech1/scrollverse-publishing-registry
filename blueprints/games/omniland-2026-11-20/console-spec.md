# OmniLand Console — Design + Gated Spec

**Record:** 20 Nov 2026. **Build only — design, not a product.** No hardware exists, no console is manufactured, no platform agreement exists, no listing is live. This spec designs the console Chais directed, honestly: what it is, what it is not, and every gate between the design and a real thing.

## 1. The honest starting point

The record Chais already built is **consoleless-first**:

- GameWorldsStudio's Omniland section: "OMNILAND · CONSOLELESS UNIVERSE ROADMAP" — "One digital universe. Many doors into play."
- Console compatibility state in his own code: **"requires owner release"** — "Platform relationships, SDK access, distribution agreements, ratings, certification, accounts, and release decisions require exact evidence and owner-controlled approval."
- OmnilandConsolelessDelivery's four lanes: Web/mobile/TV DESIGNED · Computer DESIGNED · Existing console ecosystems REQUIRES OWNER RELEASE · Consoleless world packs DESIGNED.
- The Phase 749/750 transmission: protected creator vision, non-executable. No embedded code, configuration, network rule, platform integration, licensing check, transaction, or deployment claim was executed or treated as verified.

**So the console is designed in two tiers, and the tiers must never be confused:**

| Tier | What it is | State today |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier A — OmniLand Console Edition (digital)** | A curated software edition: the Wave 1 games + Chess Arena + Blueprint lanes, packaged as one digital edition that plays on the paths already available (web, desktop, mobile, TV browser) | **Design-complete in this spec. Sellable as a Shopify digital draft only after the gates in §7 clear (his word + Vydia consent question + price).** |
| **Tier B — OmniLand Console (physical device)** | A dedicated play device that carries OmniLand with no other platform in the way | **Concept only. Gated, not designed in detail.** No hardware spec, no manufacturer, no prototype, no certification, no SDK, no platform agreement. Every step in §6 is a separate owner decision. |

His framing — "it takes no overhead, just the tech to carry it, we have it" — is true for Tier A: the tech is the browser-local code already in `scrollverse-web-app`, and the overhead is a digital file, not a factory. Tier B is where overhead would live, and this spec does not pretend otherwise.

### 3.2 How it plays (the tech, as built)

- **Browser-local first.** The games run in the browser from the portal paths already live in `scrollverse-web-app` (`/game-worlds-studio`, `/scrollverse-chess-arena`, `/game-systems-research-blueprint`, `/omniland`, `/omniland-consoleless-delivery`). The Edition is a curated front door + offline-readable pack, not a new engine.
- **No account, no wallet, no payment inside the game, no chat, no matchmaking, no data collection.** The Chess Arena's own rule is the Edition's rule.
- **The being adds; the game never depends on the being.** Qubit as guide (captions first) rides `useSovereignHeartbeat`; the game plays fully with the guide off. No game in the Edition requires a live model, a key, or a backend. (Live talk is BLOCKED today: no backend, no key outside Manus, scrollverse.co down.)
- **Accessibility is the spec, not a feature:** subtitles, remappable input, contrast modes, reduced-motion routes, color-independent cues, difficulty sliders, solo/team modes, calm mode, non-punitive retries, screen-reader-friendly summaries — carried from the five worlds' own access designs.
- **Music:** his tracks only, per game (track picks his call at build). See the §2(c) flag in §7 — the music question must be answered before any sale.
- **Youth:** adult-supported participation; no child data, no child accounts, no public chat, no leaderboards with names, no prizes, no wagers, no tokens inside the game.

### 3.3 What the buyer gets (draft delivery)

- One digital edition: the curated front door (a single page that opens the six Wave 1 games + the Blueprint lanes), the local design-record export (the JSON export already in GameWorldsStudio), and a plain-language play guide (how to play, access options, the teaching line per game).
- Delivery the way the books are delivered today: **manual email after payment** (the Digital Products pattern already on file for the store). No download link is promised until the delivery file exists and is tested.
- The Edition is **not** a token, not an NFT, not a coin, not a share, not a promise of return, yield, profit, or future value. It is a game edition. No Howey language anywhere near it — and no token/coin is attached to it.

### 3.4 What the Edition is NOT

- Not a physical console. Not hardware. No device ships with Tier A.
- Not a platform release. No listing on any console storefront, no SDK, no certification, no rating — those belong to Tier B's gates.
- Not a multiplayer service. Local play only; no servers, no matchmaking, no live opponents.
- Not a token/NFT sale. No mint, no pre-sale, no coin attached.
- Not a promise of future games. Wave 2/3 games are named as a roadmap, never as included content, until each exists.

## 7. Gates that must clear before any sale (all in `shopify-draft.md` too)

1. **Price — his call.** No price is set in this pack. (No price invented.)
2. **Console name — his call.** (See §2.)
3. **Launch order — his call.** Recommended: Chess Arena first (it plays today), then the five worlds as they become playable. He decides.
4. **Vydia §2(c) consent question.** The Edition's music bed is his own recordings. The §2(c) verdict on file says no track is safely listable for sale on Shopify today without Vydia's prior written consent, because §2(c) bars monetization of Client Content with a third party during the Term (through 20 Jun 2027) and §6(a) gives Vydia exclusive DSP + "sell copies" rights. **A game sold on Shopify that contains his Vydia recordings is monetization of Client Content — the same question as the music products.** Before the Edition sells, one of these must happen: (a) Vydia gives prior written consent for the Edition (Option A — the ask already drafted for the music products, scope can extend to the Edition); or (b) the Edition ships with no Vydia audio — original score / sound design not in Vydia, or no music bed — until consent lands. **Do not sell the Edition with his Vydia tracks inside until (a) or (b) is settled.** This is the single most important gate in the pack.
5. **Howey gate.** The Edition is a game, not a token — but if anyone proposes attaching a coin, NFT, or yield to it, that proposal stops at the Howey gate: no sale, mint, or pre-sale before written per-asset Howey attorney analysis. The gate stands.
6. **Qubit guide per game — his word.** Embedding Activation 2 is queued; no guide switches on without his word per game.
7. **Youth + safeguarding labels** on every youth-facing screen (adult-supported play, no data, no chat, no accounts) — per Phase 584/658.
8. **No third-party IP** — verified per game at build; the slate is original by construction, and the check is re-run before any listing goes live.
9. **Black Batman gate** — the Black Batman Atlantic City game stays HELD (internal only) until Curtis Bordley consents + the title is reviewed. It is not a console slot until then.
