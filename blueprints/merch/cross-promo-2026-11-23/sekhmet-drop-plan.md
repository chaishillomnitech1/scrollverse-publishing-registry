## 5. Sweepstakes law — the shape that holds (non-negotiable)

- **No purchase necessary. Free entry with equal prominence and equal odds** — the AMOE form sits on the same page as the paid entry, and free entries are indistinguishable in the draw.
- "No purchase necessary to enter or win" appears on the terms page, the entry product page, and **every** promotional post.
- A paid-only raffle is an illegal lottery; this program never runs that shape. The $1.00 entry product is an *optional* lane beside a free lane of equal standing — that is the whole legal architecture.
- Eligibility, age floor, geographic limits, draw method, winner-notification and alternate rules are all **stated in the published terms before the window opens** — never improvised after the draw.
