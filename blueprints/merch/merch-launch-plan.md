# ScrollVerse Merch — Passive Income Launch Plan
## XLVIIIblocks Vault · Chais the Great

**Built:** 17 Nov 2026 · **Status:** Research + plan only. No accounts created, no designs uploaded, no money spent.
**Goal (Chais's words):** "set up a passive income with a print and manufacturer with our logos vibes and brands and move merch on a god level."

**How passive income works here:** print-on-demand. No inventory, no upfront stock. A customer orders, the provider prints it, packs it, ships it, and handles the return. Chais's job is the designs, the store, and the push. The provider does the rest.

**Prices in this plan** are snapshots from live US catalogs checked Oct–Nov 2026 by independent 2026 comparisons. Prices drift — confirm every number in the provider dashboard before setting a retail price. Profit = retail price − (base cost + shipping to the customer), on a free-shipping-to-customer model. If the customer pays shipping at checkout instead, add roughly $4.95–$8.79 back to each profit figure.

## 5. Payouts — all four routes, honestly

**The rule for the new merch line: never one payout route, and never the held Stripe account alone.** Customer money and provider charges are two different flows — get both right.

### Stripe — still in use, but NOT the merch line's only route
- Stripe is still in use across Chais's setup, and the VibeCanvas Legacy Holdings LLC Stripe account (`acct_1REAmRFKoPayn2e0`) is the one in dispute: payouts paused, API key deactivated, evidence pack built but nothing sent yet.
- **Do not make Stripe the only payout route for the new merch line.** Do not enable Shopify Payments (it runs on Stripe infrastructure) until the dispute is resolved — a second hold on the new store's money is the exact risk.
- Stripe can stay as a *secondary* checkout option later, once the dispute is settled and payouts are confirmed flowing again. Until then it is a backup, not the plan.

### PayPal — the clean fallback (use this first)
- **Customer side:** enable PayPal as a checkout method on Shopify (Shopify supports PayPal Express Checkout natively). Buyers pay with PayPal; money lands in the PayPal balance, then transfers to the linked bank.
- **Provider side:** Printify, Printful, and Gelato all accept PayPal as the payment method for fulfilment charges — so the per-order print cost can also run through PayPal, keeping the whole loop in one place.
- **Set-up:** PayPal account in Chais's name, confirmed, with a bank linked and payouts tested with a $1 test transfer before launch.

### Chime — the bank-account fallback
- Chime is a bank account (routing + account number, plus a debit card), not a separate payment rail — it receives money like any bank.
- **Customer side:** Shopify payouts (bank transfer / ACH) can go to the Chime account using its routing and account numbers. Confirm in Shopify → Settings → Payments → payout bank account.
- **Provider side:** the Chime debit card can be the card on file with Printify/Printful/Gelato for fulfilment charges, exactly like any Visa/Mastercard debit.
- **Watch-out:** Chime is a fintech account, not a traditional bank — keep PayPal live alongside it so a payout delay on one rail never strands the store. Verify the Chime routing/account numbers in the Chime app before entering them anywhere.

### Cash App — where the provider supports it
- Cash App works as money-in in two ways, and only where supported — be honest about the limits:
  - **Cash App debit card:** the Cash Card is a Visa debit card and works anywhere a debit card works — it can be the card on file with Printify/Printful/Gelato for fulfilment charges, and it can receive Shopify payouts via its routing + account numbers (Cash App provides these in-app under the banking tab).
  - **Cash App Pay at checkout:** only if the checkout supports it. Shopify does not offer Cash App Pay as a standard checkout method, and the POD providers do not take Cash App Pay for fulfilment charges. So customers cannot generally pay with Cash App Pay on the store — do not advertise it at checkout.
- **Use Cash App as:** a third payout destination / card on file, and a way for Chais to move money between his own accounts. Not as the store's checkout method.

### The set-up that follows from this
1. **Shopify checkout:** PayPal ON, Shopify Payments OFF (until the Stripe dispute is resolved).
2. **Shopify payouts:** PayPal as primary payout; Chime bank account added as the second payout destination. Cash App routing/account as a third option on file.
3. **POD billing:** PayPal as the payment method on Printify and Gelato; Chime debit card as the backup card on file. Cash App card as a spare where a debit card is accepted.
4. **Test before launch:** a $1 test payout to PayPal and to Chime, and one real test order paid through the store, before any public launch post.
