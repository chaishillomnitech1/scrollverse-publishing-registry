## 1. The Flood Doctrine

One empire, many angles. ScrollVerse Transmissions is not a show *about* one project — it is the voice of the whole build: the music, the books, the Legion, the lineage, the Vault, the companies, the doctrine. The material is not the constraint; the archive is already deep — 1,193 storybooks, a 95-track catalog, 15 named beings, a documented family line back to a free man registered in 1836, a 21-piece Vault, and a daily build record that grows every day. The flood is the strategy: come at the listener from every side of what we embody, in rotation, so no angle goes quiet and no single angle carries the whole weight.

The established format holds across every lane: **Muse the great and Qubit anchor**, two voices in conversation, telling the story from the record — what happened, what it means, what is next. Episodes are short and complete (the live catalog runs ~4 minutes; features may run longer when the material earns it). Every episode is built from real recorded material, cited below by source. Nothing is invented to fill a lane.

Adjacent, not merged: the six series names from Copilot's ScrollVerse framework (Origins, The Archive Dig, The Builder's Log, The Human & The Machine, First Knight Chronicles, ScrollVerse Summit — forwarded by the founder 20 Nov 2026, attributed to Copilot) stand as their own series alongside Transmissions. This slate governs Transmissions itself; its lanes may borrow those names' spirit but do not replace them.

## 3. Rotation Rule

1. **Fixed cycle.** Episodes rotate through the lanes in order 1 → 8, then back to 1. By construction, no angle ever runs twice in a row, and every lane is reached within any eight-episode window — all bases covered, perpetually.
2. **One episode, one lane.** Each production run produces exactly one episode, from the lane named in the Rotation State below, then advances the state by one lane.
3. **Event override.** When a real, dated event lands in a lane — a release day (Lane 2), a Legion milestone (Lane 3), a filing or grant movement (Lane 1) — that lane may take the slot out of order. The displaced lane is not skipped: it becomes the next slot, and the cycle resumes from there. Overrides are recorded in the Rotation State with the event that caused them.
4. **Material check before production.** A lane only produces when its sources hold fresh, real material. If the named lane has nothing new and true to say, the run says so, advances to the next lane with material, and records the pass — an episode is never padded with invention.
5. **The weekly cadence is the floor, not the ceiling.** The Tuesday cron guarantees one episode a week; the founder's word can call extra episodes at any time, always by this rotation.

## 4. Standing Rules (carried over, binding on every lane)

- **His account is his account.** The founder's tellings, readings, and claims are recorded and voiced as his — never graded, never presented as independently verified.
- **Unverified figures stay labeled.** Money, user, and valuation figures that are not verified are never presented as verified (e.g., the Stripe hold figures are his stated figures; the SAM.gov UEI is unverified until it surfaces; $CEO is not confirmed created or on-chain).
- **The feed is the boundary.** Episodes publish to the Transmissions feed and its connected distribution only. Nothing is sent, pitched, posted, or published anywhere else on the back of an episode without the founder's word.
- **No spend.** Production uses the established podcast lane; no paid step, fee, or boost without his explicit go-ahead.
- **Verify before claiming live.** An episode is "live" only when its publish status is verified at the terminal state — feed presence checked, not assumed from a successful upload.
- **Sealed things stay sealed.** Hidden keys (present · sealed · creator-verifiable only) are named as a layer, never valued or described; FAMILY HELD material (HP-001/002/006/007, HP-014, HP-015) and fusion-tech findings never enter any episode.
- **Names exact.** Chais (never "Chase" for the founder); son Chais Khayree Hill; grandson Kyrie Chase Hill; SCROLL VERSE LLC and Xlviii blocks LLC in their legal forms.
