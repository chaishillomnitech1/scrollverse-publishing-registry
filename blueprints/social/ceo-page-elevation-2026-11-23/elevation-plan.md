## 3. Content system

### The beat, fused with the flood
The Page's standing beat (set 22 Nov): **the chosen, political news, ET, streamers, viral videos** — fused with the house's own output into five pillars:

1. **The Signal** — the beat itself: the chosen, political news, ET. Commentary and reaction in the Page's voice; the reason a stranger follows.
2. **The Screen** — streamers and viral clips: the clipping lane, riding what the culture is already watching back to the house.
3. **The Catalog** — the flood: the film/episode/clip library (Waves 1–3; 33 of 52 Wave 3 pieces scheduled, Run 4 finishes the 52 tomorrow).
4. **The Work** — the house building in public: seals, sets, drops, releases. THE CEO SET is the founding exhibit.
5. **The Counter** — revenue posts: store, Vault, books. Minimum one per day, rotated, always ending at a live door.

### Cadence
Four slots a day, ET: **10:00 · 13:30 · 17:00 · 20:00**. While the flood runs, the Catalog holds the slots in wave order; the Counter takes one slot a day (rotating 13:30/17:00); the Signal and the Screen enter as they happen — news does not wait for a wave, but it never displaces a scheduled flood piece; it takes the next open slot.

### The mesh rule (absolute)
Only scored/finished versions post — music and video mesh perfectly, verified by ffprobe (h264 + AAC) immediately before scheduling. Never an unscored alternate, never a segment, never a working cut. Holds stand: the two swoosh-mark clips and all Tier 3 material never post without Chais's explicit call (queue.md holds list governs).

### Post style (house law)
One sentence, one named thing, one link, max 3 hashtags. Core tag set: #scrollverse #chaisthegreat #XLVIIIblocks. The music speaks for itself — no trash talk, no hype figures, no unverified numbers in any caption.
