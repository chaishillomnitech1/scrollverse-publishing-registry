# 3. THE CROSS-PROMOTION LATTICE — any door opens the whole house

**The one-hop rule:** from ANY recovered surface — owned, appearance, or witness — a listener reaches the whole empire in one hop, because every controllable page carries the link-out set (§2.4) and every uncontrollable page is *cited from* controllable pages with its context attached.

**The old room hears the new record first.** The 808 followers of chaisthegreat1 are the launch audience for the 49 — they followed the songs the first time, in the years the songs were made. The re-release protocol, per recovered song, once its nine gates close and its Vydia date is set:

1. **chaisthegreat1 posts first** (once recovered): the re-release announced in the room where the original still lives, the original track pinned beside the announcement — 2013 and its official return, side by side.
2. **Every owned surface mirrors the same day:** chais-hill, Audiomack, ReverbNation, and the DistroKid doors carry the announcement with the song's Smart Link; scrollverse.co's pages add the song to the exchange.
3. **The appearance surfaces get the ask:** MaxOutTV/Xburna-side posts or description updates requested through the relationships (founder's word per ask) — the video that carried the song points at the song's official return.
4. **The witnesses do their work silently:** the registry row, the Panlogos enumeration, and the forum witness are what journalists, collaborators, and the curious find when the announcement makes them search — the proof layer under the promotion.
5. **The current wave yields the slot:** the re-released song takes the pinned/featured position (§2.6) across owned surfaces for its window, then joins the permanent link-out rotation.

**Reverse flow:** every NEW release (the Fifteen today, each wave after) is also announced into the recovered rooms once they are established — the archive audience is a standing audience for the whole catalog, not a one-time launch. Eyes and ears, both directions, permanently.
