## §0. What "deploy" means here

Three different things are all called "series" in the record, and they deploy differently:

1. **Finished clips** — 10-second AI visual clips in hand as files. These can go to YouTube now.
2. **Treatments / screenplays** — written stories with shot lists. These need video made first (a production brief from Chais, then generation, then assembly).
3. **Code in story** — technical artifacts embedded inside the stories (JSON manifests, workflows, configs, contract text). These are the "hidden tech keys": they are **written inside the fiction**, not working systems. Nothing in this map claims a story file proves a working technology.
