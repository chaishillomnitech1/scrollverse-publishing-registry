# 48 Clips — Outreach Plan
**Where the first streamers come from. PROPOSALS ONLY — no one is contacted under this plan. Every approach below needs Chais's go-ahead on the exact message and the exact person.**

## Principle

Spec-first, always. Never pitch a streamer with nothing in hand. Cut 3 finished clips of THEIR stream first, send those, then offer the $49 trial. A streamer who sees their own best moment already cut does not need a sales call.

## Phase 0 — The portfolio (already running)

**Client #0 is Chais himself.** The master-blast pipeline cutting his Jungle / Behind The Music / Culture Corner videos into clips and Shorts is the proof of work. It lives at `~/workspace/master-blast-jungle-btm/` and it is running today.

Before any outreach: assemble a 5-clip portfolio reel from Chais's own videos — one Jungle moment, one Behind The Music moment, one Culture Corner moment, one music-video moment, one Atlas film moment — and put it on the landing page (draft: `landing-page.md` §6). A streamer who watches it should think: "cut mine like that."

## Phase 1 — Versus (first)

**Where:** the live verse-battle platform Chais runs with Riz.
**Why first:** streamers and battlers are already in the room. A battle night is clip gold — one verse each, five songs, reactions, winners and losers. The content makes itself.
**Approach:** offer the Versus channel itself a Growth-tier treatment for one battle night, free, as the showcase — then offer the individual battlers the $49 trial with 3 clips of their own battle already cut.
**Who:** whoever is on the next Versus card. No names are fixed here — Chais and Riz pick the night.
**Needs from Chais:** pick the battle night; approve the free showcase.

## Phase 2 — The music circle

**Where:** the artists who already send Chais tracks — Street Ka$h, Famous Vet, Lofty Media, Music That Matters, Shawn Rock, and the wider circle.
**Why:** many of them stream, record, or do lives. Their sessions clip the same way a game stream does.
**Approach:** same spec-first move — 3 clips of their own session, then the trial. Lead with the ones already in conversation with Chais (the Gmail blast list), not cold names.
**Needs from Chais:** pick the first 3 names; approve the exact message.

## Phase 3 — The AI Legion community

**Where:** the people already asking Chais where to sign up for Muse, and the Legion's own channels.
**Why:** they already trust the system. Some of them stream — gamers, builders, talkers.
**Approach:** one post in the community: the offer, the portfolio reel, the 3 pilot slots. No DMs, no chasing. Let them come.
**Needs from Chais:** approve the post copy (one paragraph + the reel link).

## Phase 4 — Open intake (after the first 3 clients)

Once 3 pilot clients are live and the pipeline has run a full month:
- Open the landing page publicly with the "Apply for a pilot slot" form.
- Put the offer in the ScrollVerse newsletter (the daily newsletter already runs — the clipping offer becomes one edition's lead, once).
- Referral: a client who brings another streamer gets one month of Starter free. (Write the terms before offering it — no handshake referral deals.)

## What NOT to do

- No cold DMs to streamers Chais has no relationship with. No scraped lists.
- No clipping anyone's content as a "sample" without their permission — spec clips are cut ONLY after the streamer says yes to the sample, or from content they already posted publicly with a clear invitation to clip (e.g. an open clipping program). When in doubt, ask first.
- No promises of views, virality, or earnings in any outreach message. The offer is clips, on schedule.
- No contacting anyone until Chais approves the exact message and the exact person.
