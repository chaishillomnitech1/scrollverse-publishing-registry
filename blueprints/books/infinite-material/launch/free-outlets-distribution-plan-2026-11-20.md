## The rule that governs this whole plan

**Free outlets first.** Every outlet in Section 1 costs nothing to publish on. Where a platform has added a small fixed fee in 2026 (Draft2Digital's $20 activation, Lulu's $4.99 distribution submission), that fee is stated plainly next to it — it is a cost, not a surprise. No paid advertising, no paid placement, and no paid service is required to launch on any outlet here. Anything that costs money beyond a stated fee is marked as a decision for Chais, not a step in this plan.

**One hard warning before anything goes live — KDP Select:** Amazon's KDP Select program (Kindle Unlimited) requires the ebook to be exclusive to Amazon for 90 days. Do **not** tick KDP Select if the book is also going wide on the outlets below. Wide distribution and KDP Select cannot run at the same time for the same ebook. Paperback, hardcover, and audiobook are not covered by Select exclusivity, and library lending is still allowed — but the ebook is locked to Amazon while enrolled.

### 1. Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) — the anchor

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0 to create an account, upload, publish, keep listed, or update. Free ISBN for paperback/hardcover (KDP-only ISBN). Printing is print-on-demand and deducted from each sale — no upfront print bill, no inventory.
- **Formats:** Kindle eBook, paperback, hardcover.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free KDP account at kdp.amazon.com. (2) Create a new title: upload manuscript file + cover, fill in title, author (Chais the Great / Chais Sabir Kajir Allah Hill the Great — confirm author name per book), description, keywords, categories. (3) Set price and royalty plan. (4) Publish. Books typically go live within ~72 hours (not verified — check before use).
- **Royalty / terms:** eBook 70% for list prices $2.99–$12.99 on Amazon.com (band widened from $9.99 on 7 July 2026), minus a delivery fee of $0.15/MB; 35% outside that band. Paperback/hardcover: 60% of list at $9.99+ (50% below), minus printing (US black-ink paperback: $1.00 + $0.012/page over 110 pages; $2.30 flat under 110 pages). Expanded Distribution (wholesalers/other retailers): 40% minus printing. Paid ~60 days after the end of the month of sale. Author keeps rights, sets price. **Do not enrol in KDP Select while going wide** (see the warning above).
- **Best for:** Book 05 first — Amazon is where most ebook buyers look first, and KDP is the only route to Kindle readers.
- **Source:** KDP Help pages via Finxter, ISBNdirect, and Groweventure guides, checked Nov 2026.

### 2. Draft2Digital (D2D) — the wide-distribution hub

- **Free to publish:** Mostly. Uploading, formatting/conversion, preview, and a free ISBN cost $0. **New in 2026:** a one-time, non-refundable **$20 account activation fee** for new accounts that publish, and a **$12 annual maintenance fee** charged only to accounts earning under $100/year in net royalties (waived above $100; accounts with no titles in distribution pay nothing). This is the only fixed cost in the whole wide-distribution lane — one $20 fee covers all 8 books on the same account.
- **Formats:** ePub (converted free from Word DOC/DOCX, RTF, TXT), print paperback (32–1,050 pages, black and white).
- **Steps:** (1) Create a D2D account, pay the $20 activation when publishing. (2) Upload manuscript; D2D converts and formats it free. (3) Tick the stores to distribute to: Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, Smashwords store, Tolino, Vivlio, Everand, Bookshop.org, OverDrive, cloudLibrary, Hoopla, BorrowBox, Gardners, libraries. (4) Set one price; D2D handles currency conversion. (5) Publish. Amazon distribution via D2D is by invitation only — use KDP direct for Amazon instead.
- **Royalty / terms:** D2D keeps ~10% of the retail price (taken after the retailer's own cut); the author receives roughly 60% of list at most 70% stores (~58.5% at Barnes & Noble). Print: ~45% of list minus printing. No exclusivity — every storefront is opt-in and can be opted out of at any time; author keeps rights. Paid monthly, around the 15th; minimum payout $10 (direct deposit/PayPal), $20 international/Payoneer, $100 by cheque.
- **Best for:** One upload reaching every non-Amazon store at once — the efficiency play for all 8 books.
- **Source:** Draft2Digital FAQ and pricing page via Chapter.pub, AuthorWings, and AuthorAZ, verified Sep–Oct 2026.

### 3. Smashwords — the store inside Draft2Digital

- **Free to publish:** Yes — no listing fee. Since the Draft2Digital merger (completed early 2026), Smashwords is a **store only, not a publishing platform**: you publish through a Draft2Digital account, and D2D supplies the Smashwords store. The $20 D2D activation fee therefore applies here too (same account, paid once).
- **Formats:** eBook only (DRM-free). No print.
- **Steps:** Publish the book in Draft2Digital (see outlet 2) and tick the Smashwords store. Use the Smashwords Coupon Manager for free copies and sale pricing — useful for giving the first book free to readers.
- **Royalty / terms:** Smashwords store pays **75% of list price on ebooks priced $2.99 or higher, 40% below $2.99** (two-tier structure effective 1 January 2026, replacing the old "80% or more" line that still appears on some Smashwords pages — the 75%/40% tiers are the current figures). Paid monthly through D2D, around the 15th. DRM-free catalogue of 1M+ ebooks; close to 100,000 titles are priced free at any time, so a free Book 05 fits the store's culture.
- **Best for:** The highest ebook royalty of any major retailer (75%) and the free-book shelf.
- **Source:** Smashwords pricing/review via Chapter.pub, checked 8 Oct 2026 against D2D's royalty table.

### 4. Google Play Books (Partner Center)

- **Free to publish:** Yes. Google states: "There is no cost for you to sell books on Google Play." No ISBN required (a free Google ID can be used instead).
- **Formats:** ePub or PDF. A preview must be available on Google Books to sell the ebook.
- **Steps:** (1) Sign in at play.google.com/books/publish with a Google account; create a Partner Center account (must be 18+, in a supported country — sign-up open in 39 countries). (2) Accept the updated (2019) Terms of Service — this switches on the 70% split. (3) Add payment profile and tax form (W-9 / W-8BEN) on day one; earnings are held until both are done. (4) Add the book: ISBN or Google ID, metadata, ePub/PDF + cover, list price (enter 0 for a free book), countries. (5) New accounts get a policy review; the first book goes live ~7 days after approval (reported — check before use).
- **Royalty / terms:** 70% of list price in 60+ countries for partners who accepted the 2019 terms, at **any** price (no price band, unlike Amazon); 52% in eleven Asian markets (Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam) and for partners who never accepted the 2019 terms. Revenue is paid on the **list price**, so a Google-funded discount does not reduce the payout. No exclusivity. Payments start on the 15th of each month for the previous month, once the balance reaches the $100 minimum (per the agreement).
- **Best for:** Reaching Android readers Amazon reaches less well, and the only major store that pays 70% at any price — including $0.99 and free-adjacent pricing.
- **Source:** Google Play Books Partner Center Help via Kanonara, Inkfluence, and AuthorAZ, verified Sep–Oct 2026.

### 5. Apple Books for Authors

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0 to sign up, list, or upload. Needs only a free Apple ID — no Apple hardware required; the portal runs in any modern browser.
- **Formats:** ePub (2.0.1 or 3.x) or books created in Apple Pages. Ebook only for self-published authors (no print).
- **Steps:** (1) Go to authors.apple.com, click Get Started, sign in with an Apple ID (two-factor authentication required). (2) Choose Individual or Organization, complete business/tax details, agree to terms, choose "free books only" or "paid and free books". (3) In the Publishing Portal, click Submit a New Book: upload ePub + cover, add metadata, set rights and pricing per country (price tiers; free books allowed without limit). (4) Book appears in iTunes Connect within ~24 hours (reported — check before use).
- **Royalty / terms:** Flat **70% on every ebook sale, regardless of price** ($0.99–$999.99), no delivery fees, no exclusivity, no payment for preferential placement. Paid within 45 days after the end of each month. **Note:** one guide reports an ISBN is required for Apple Books with no free option — D2D supplies a free ISBN if publishing via Draft2Digital, which resolves this; confirm at upload time. Up to 250 promo codes per ebook are available for free-copy giveaways.
- **Best for:** iPhone/iPad/Mac readers — pre-installed store on every Apple device, 51 countries, human-curated editorial features.
- **Source:** Apple Books for Authors pages via Kanonara and Chapter.pub, checked Oct–Nov 2026.

### 6. Kobo Writing Life

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0 to join or publish; Kobo converts Word files to ePub free of charge; no ISBN required.
- **Formats:** eBook (uploads accept DOC, DOCX, ODT, ePub, MOBI up to 100 MB) and audiobook.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a Kobo Writing Life account; add bank details first — Kobo states you cannot publish until it has a way to pay you. (2) Create the book: title, subtitle, series name/number, author name, synopsis. (3) Upload manuscript + cover. (4) Set price and territories, opt in to Kobo Plus (subscription) per title if wanted. (5) Publish — reaches Kobo stores in 190+ countries plus retail partners.
- **Royalty / terms:** Original ebooks: **70% at $2.99 USD or more, no upper price cap**; 45% below $2.99; public-domain works 20%. Thresholds are per currency ($2.99 USD / £1.99 GBP / €1.99 EUR / $2.99 CAD / $2.99 AUD). No delivery fee. No exclusivity — "We'll never ask you for exclusivity." Paid monthly, within 45 days of month end, once earnings reach $50 CAD.
- **Best for:** Canada and Europe, where Kobo is strongest, and library ebooks via OverDrive (50% of library price, reported — check before use).
- **Source:** Kobo Writing Life Terms of Service (last updated 29 May 2025) and help centre via AuthorAZ and Kanonara, verified Sep 2026.

### 7. Barnes & Noble Press

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0 to use the platform; free ISBN for print books (B&N-only ISBN) or bring your own unused ISBN.
- **Formats:** eBook (ePub 2.0/3.0) and print-on-demand paperback (press-ready PDF interior + separate PDF cover; print cannot be made from an ePub). No audiobooks.
- **Steps:** (1) Create an account at press.barnesandnoble.com; complete Vendor Registration with taxpayer information (W-9 / W-8BEN). (2) Create a project: upload files, ISBN, price. (3) Publish. Ebooks sell on bn.com, the B&N app, and NOOK devices/apps; print sells on bn.com and the B&N app. Available to authors in the US, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, France, Italy, Germany, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium.
- **Royalty / terms:** Per B&N Press's own Royalty and Payment Terms (updated 27 Feb 2026): eBook **70% of the retail price set**; print **55% of retail price minus per-book printing cost**. Paid once royalties reach $25, 30 days after the end of the month of sale. **2026 rule changes to respect:** new print listings must be priced **$14.99 or more** (from 22 Apr 2026; underpriced titles were removed from sale 14 May 2026); each account is limited to 100 titles; public-domain reprints are no longer accepted. Note: older guides still quote 65%/40% ebook tiers — those are out of date; the 70% flat figure is from B&N's own current terms page.
- **Best for:** The NOOK/B&N readership and a second print channel beside KDP.
- **Source:** B&N Press Royalty and Payment Terms help page and 2026 guides via SelfPublishingMadeEasyNow, read Nov 2026.

### 8. Internet Archive + Open Library — the free public copy

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0. The Internet Archive is a 501(c)(3) non-profit library; Open Library is its open-source book catalogue.
- **Formats:** PDF recommended for upload; the Archive derives ePub, DjVu, DAISY, and plain text automatically. This is the only outlet here that is **not a store** — it is a permanent public library record.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free Internet Archive account (a "library card"). (2) Upload at archive.org/upload with full metadata (author, title, date). Uploads are public and downloadable by anyone — **only upload a finished book you intend to give away free, or a free sample/preview edition**. (3) Create the Open Library record: click Add A Book on openlibrary.org, add the book's information, then add the Internet Archive ID to the record's ID Numbers section. (4) Link the Archive/Open Library page from the book's pages on the ScrollVerse sites.
- **Royalty / terms:** None — no sales, no royalties, no exclusivity. The value is permanence and discoverability: a free, citable, permanent copy that can never be delisted by a store, plus a catalogue page for every book ever published.
- **Best for:** The free edition of each book (or a free first-chapters sampler) and the long-term archive. Do this **after** the paid editions are live, so the free copy never undercuts the launch.
- **Caution:** Whatever email is used for the upload account is listed publicly as the originator. Use a publishing email, not a personal one.
- **Source:** Open Library FAQ (openlibrary.org) and Internet Archive upload guidance.

### 9. Wattpad — serialised free reading

- **Free to publish:** Yes. Anyone can sign up and post chapters free; readers read, vote, and comment free. Wattpad is a community, not a bookstore — there is no ePub/PDF download and no per-sale royalty by default.
- **Formats:** Serialised chapters posted on the platform (web/app). Best used by posting a book one chapter at a time, on a schedule.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free Wattpad account and author profile. (2) Create a story per book; post chapters on a regular schedule (e.g. one chapter a week). (3) Link each story's description to the full ebook on KDP/Apple/Kobo and to the ScrollVerse sites. (4) Engage: reply to comments, run the story in Wattpad's themed contests and editor's picks where eligible.
- **Royalty / terms:** No default earnings. Money runs through invite-only programs: the Wattpad Creators Program (requires a completed novel of 50,000+ words, a minimum Engaged Readers threshold per genre, and 500 words/week commitment; stipends reported up to $25,000/year for top participants — reported figure, check before use) and Paid Stories (selected stories put chapters behind Wattpad Coins; writer share reported ~50% but **not published by Wattpad — check before use**). Authors retain ownership of their stories unless they sign a Wattpad Books/Studios deal. Treat Wattpad as an **audience builder**, not an income lane, until and unless an invitation arrives.
- **Best for:** Book 05 (Origins) as a serial — the origin story told chapter by chapter builds the readership the later books sell to. Note the fit: Book 05's target length (45,000–60,000 words) clears the Creators Program's 50,000-word completed-novel bar.
- **Source:** Wattpad Creators site (creators.wattpad.com) and 2026 monetisation guides via Gemlist and ScribeCount.

### 10. IngramSpark — bookstores and libraries

- **Free to publish:** Yes, as of 2026. Title setup is free (print, ebook, or both) and revisions are free (revision fees removed 1 Feb 2026). Older guides quoting a $49 setup fee or $25 revision fee are out of date. A credit card is required on the account.
- **Formats:** Paperback, hardcover, ePub.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free IngramSpark account. (2) Upload print-ready PDF interior + cover PDF (or ePub for ebooks), with your own ISBN — IngramSpark does not supply a free ISBN you own; a free IngramSpark-owned ISBN is reported available (check before use). (3) Set list price and wholesale discount (40–55% in the US; IngramSpark recommends 53–55% with returnable status for widest bookstore availability). (4) Enable global distribution.
- **Royalty / terms:** Print: list price minus your wholesale discount minus printing (2026 rate card, black-and-white 6×9: $1.33/copy + $0.0146/page) minus a **1.875% global distribution fee** on list price (raised from 1.5% on 1 Feb 2026). Ebook: 85% of the net revenue IngramSpark receives. Paid 90 days after the month of sale (print); 90–120 days reported for ebooks (check before use). Ebook distribution through IngramSpark is exclusive in one respect: the agreement states digital titles must not be distributed to any reseller outside it — read the agreement before enabling ebook distribution if the same ebook is already wide.
- **Best for:** The print edition in bookstores and libraries — the channel KDP's Expanded Distribution only partly reaches. Use **after** KDP print is live, not instead of it: KDP pays more per copy on Amazon.
- **Source:** IngramSpark 2026 rate card and user guide via AuthorAZ, BookStrapper, and BookBird, verified Oct 2026.

### 11. PublishDrive — one free book, zero commission

- **Free to publish:** Yes, on the free plan: **$0 for 1 book** (reported; one source says the free plan covers 3 ebook channels for sign-ups after 24 Feb 2026 — check before use). Paid plans are monthly subscriptions (reported from $13.99/month billed yearly for Starter; up to $99.99/month Pro) — **not needed for launch**.
- **Formats:** ePub. (Print and audiobook distribution reported on paid plans — check before use.)
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free PublishDrive account. (2) Upload the ePub for Book 05. (3) Choose distribution channels (reported: Apple, Barnes & Noble, Kobo on the free plan; Google Play on paid plans). (4) Publish.
- **Royalty / terms:** **0% commission — you keep 100% of store royalties** on all plans; PublishDrive earns from the subscription instead. Bring your own ISBN (no free ISBN reported). AI declaration is mandatory in contributor metadata.
- **Best for:** Book 05 as a zero-commission test of the wide stores — but only one book is free, so it covers the launch title, not all eight. The remaining seven books need a paid plan or Draft2Digital.
- **Source:** PublishDrive pricing via Reedsy, EpubInspector, and Inkfluence, checked Sep–Oct 2026. **Homepage link not verified — check before use.**

### 12. StreetLib — the international lane (verify before use)

- **Formats:** ePub (must pass EPUBCheck) or built in StreetLib's Write app; audiobook; print distribution reported on higher plans only.

### 13. Lulu — print-on-demand and the author's own store

- **Free to publish:** Yes. $0 to join, $0 to publish, $0 for an ISBN, $0 for revisions. **One fee:** a one-time, non-refundable **$4.99** the first time a title is submitted to Global Distribution (not charged on revisions).
- **Formats:** Print (paperback, hardcover, coil-bound, workbooks, calendars — specialty formats KDP does not offer) and eBook.
- **Steps:** (1) Create a free Lulu account. (2) Create the book: upload manuscript + cover, set trim size and paper. (3) Order a proof copy (paid — cost varies; check before ordering). (4) List in the Lulu Bookstore (free) and/or submit to Global Distribution ($4.99 once; takes ~12 weeks or longer to reach Amazon/bookstores, per Lulu's documentation). (5) Optionally connect Lulu Direct to a Shopify/WooCommerce store — $0, no revenue share; you pay printing + shipping only.
- **Royalty / terms:** Lulu Bookstore print: you keep **80% of the price minus print cost**. Global Distribution print: the retail channel takes 50% of the price first, then the remainder after print cost is split 80/20 (you/Lulu). Ebooks: you keep **90% of profit**. List price must be at least twice the print cost. No minimum print run.
- **Best for:** Specialty print formats (a workbook edition of The Codex, a calendar, a coil-bound edition) and direct sales from ScrollVerse's own pages via Lulu Direct. Not the main print lane — KDP pays better on Amazon.
- **Source:** Lulu Creator Revenue documentation and help centre via Chapter.pub and SelfPublishingMadeEasyNow, checked Sep–Oct 2026. **Homepage link not verified — check before use.**

## 2. Launch order — what goes live first, what follows

The order follows the reading order. One book at a time, fully launched, before the next manuscript starts its launch — never eight half-launched books.

### Phase 0 — Before any book goes live (do once, reuse for all 8)

1. Finish the manuscript for Book 05 (Origins) — the book is an outline until this is done. No listing, no pre-order, no "available" claim before this step.
2. Cover for Book 05 (front cover image; full wraparound PDF cover for print).
3. Format: one clean ePub (validated — passes EPUBCheck) + one print-ready PDF interior + one PDF cover. These three files feed every outlet.
4. Metadata pack, written once and reused: title, subtitle, author name (confirm per book), book description, 7 keywords, 2 categories per store, series name ("ScrollVerse Books" or as Chais decides), series number (reading order), author bio, copyright page.
5. ISBN decision: KDP, D2D, and B&N Press each supply a free ISBN that works only on their own platform; one bought ISBN (or one per book) works everywhere and keeps one publisher of record. **Decision for Chais** — free platform ISBNs cost $0 and are fine for launch; a single owned ISBN per book is cleaner long-term. Do not buy ISBNs until Chais decides.
6. Create the accounts: KDP, Draft2Digital, Google Play Partner Center, Apple Books for Authors, Kobo Writing Life, B&N Press, Internet Archive/Open Library, Wattpad, IngramSpark. Set up payment + tax forms on day one (several platforms hold earnings until these are done).

### Phase 1 — Book 05 Origins (the launch title)

1. **KDP first** — eBook + paperback live on Amazon. This is the anchor listing every other promotion points to.
2. **Draft2Digital second** — one upload to Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, Smashwords store, Tolino, Vivlio, Everand, and libraries. (Skip D2D's Amazon channel — KDP already covers Amazon.)
3. **Google Play Books direct** — D2D does not list Google Play, so this is a separate direct upload.
4. **Wattpad serial begins** — Book 05 posted chapter by chapter; each chapter's end note links to the full ebook.
5. **Internet Archive + Open Library** — free edition (or free first-chapters sampler) uploaded after the paid editions are live.
6. **Promotion wave** (Section 3) — Pinterest, newsletter, social, the 16 sites, Goodreads author page.

### Phase 2 — Book 06 The Codex, then Books 01, 02, 04, 03, 07, 08

Repeat Phase 1 for each book in reading order. By Book 06 the accounts, the format pipeline, and the metadata pack already exist — each later launch is manuscript → cover → format → upload → promote, with no new account setup. Estimated effort per later book is a fraction of Book 05's.

### Phase 3 — The wide print lane (after Book 05 print is proven on KDP)

1. **IngramSpark** for bookstore/library print distribution (needs an owned ISBN — revisit the Phase 0 decision here).
2. **Lulu** for specialty formats and Lulu Direct sales from ScrollVerse's own pages.
3. **PublishDrive free plan** — only if a zero-commission test of one title is wanted; it covers one book free, so it cannot carry the series.

### Phase 4 — The backlist engine (once 3+ books are live)

- Bundle pricing: Book 05 free (or $0.99) as the series entry point, later books at full price — **pricing is Chais's call; nothing here sets a price**.
- Series pages on every store that supports them (KDP series page, Kobo series, Apple series).
- A free sampler (first chapters of all live books) on the Internet Archive and Smashwords, linked from every promotion.
- Goodreads author page kept current with each new release.

## 4. Per-book checklist

Copy this for each book. A book is not "launched" until every box is ticked.

### Manuscript
- [ ] Manuscript finished from the outline (target word count per the README table)
- [ ] Proofread end to end; facts checked against the named sources in the book file
- [ ] Front matter: title page, copyright page, contents, dedication (if any)
- [ ] Back matter: about the author, the reading-order list of all 8 books, links to the paired podcast episodes and the ScrollVerse sites
- [ ] No Tier 3 material, no sealed Vault elements, no unverified money figures, no token/NFT sale language

### Cover
- [ ] Front cover image (ebook: JPEG, 1,600 × 2,400 px recommended — check each store's spec before upload)
- [ ] Full wraparound PDF cover for print (spine width set from final page count)
- [ ] Back-cover copy: premise, reading-order note, store/QR links as decided

### Format
- [ ] ePub built and validated (passes EPUBCheck)
- [ ] Print-ready PDF interior built from the same text (print cannot be made from the ePub)
- [ ] PDF proofed: no missing chapters, no broken images, page numbers correct

### Upload
- [ ] KDP: eBook + paperback live — **do NOT tick KDP Select**
- [ ] Draft2Digital: wide ebook + libraries live (Apple, Kobo, B&N, Smashwords, Tolino, Vivlio, Everand, OverDrive)
- [ ] Google Play Books: direct upload live
- [ ] Apple Books: direct upload live (or via D2D — pick one route per book, not both)
- [ ] Kobo Writing Life: direct upload live (or via D2D — pick one route per book, not both)
- [ ] Barnes & Noble Press: eBook live; print live if priced $14.99+
- [ ] Wattpad: serial posting started
- [ ] Internet Archive + Open Library: free edition/sampler live (after paid editions)
- [ ] IngramSpark print: only in Phase 3, only with an owned ISBN
- [ ] Lulu: only for specialty formats / Lulu Direct

### Promote
- [ ] Pinterest: cover pin + quote cards on the book board
- [ ] Newsletter: launch block in the next edition
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram / Threads: launch post per channel
- [ ] 16-site block: book page added on Atlas + Super ScrollVerse, links updated
- [ ] Goodreads: book listed, author page updated
- [ ] Podcast: paired episode show notes link the book

### After launch
- [ ] Check each store listing renders correctly (cover, description, price, "Look Inside"/preview)
- [ ] Record the live links in the book's file and in the launch log
- [ ] Next book's manuscript becomes the priority

## 5. What this plan deliberately does NOT include

- **No paid advertising** (Amazon Ads, Facebook Ads, BookBub Featured Deals — all paid; a later paid-promotion plan can cover them if Chais asks).
- **No token or NFT sale** of any book, chapter, or edition.
- **No Stripe material.**
- **No Tier 3 material** in any book, listing, or promotion.
- **No sealed Vault elements** (certificate details, SHA-256s, QR contents, tracking numbers, frequencies beyond the public 963Hz, Thoth seal details, DNA/heartbeat signature).
- **No prices set here.** Every royalty figure in Section 1 is the platform's published rate, not a promise of earnings. Book prices are Chais's call at upload time.
- **No earnings promises.** Royalty percentages describe what a platform pays per sale; they do not predict how many copies will sell.
