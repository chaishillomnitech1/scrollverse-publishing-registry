## 1. THE COMPOSITION ANGLE — the loophole, stated plainly

Most authors have one income lane per book: book sales. Chais has a second lane most authors never think of, because most authors do not own a music catalog and a publishing company. The angle is this:

**The book is not only a book. Every audio expression of the book carries original music composed by Chais, registered with ASCAP under IPI 1247873912, published by his own XLVIIIBlock LLC. So the book earns as a composition everywhere its audio plays — on DSPs, in podcasts, in audiobooks, in film/TV/ads via sync — on top of whatever the book itself earns as a book.**

Four mechanisms, each real and each already half-built:

### 1A. The book has a soundtrack — and the soundtrack is already releasing

Each book gets companion music: songs and compositions that carry the book's story, released as Vydia music singles to 63–64 DSPs, with the publishing record attached. The soundtrack travels as music even before the manuscript is finished — a reader can hear Book 03 before they can read it.

- The 15 singles landing **Fri 27 Nov 2026** are the first soundtrack wave. They are already in Vydia, ISRCs + UPCs assigned. Nothing to do but watch them land and grab every Smart Link.
- The released catalog (35 Chais albums in Vydia, 49 albums / 95 tracks / 41 videos in the account) is the back-catalog soundtrack — already live on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, TIDAL, Deezer, TikTok, Facebook/Instagram.
- The soundtrack is a **music** release. That is the verified Vydia lane. Vydia is a music distributor; it does **not** take text books, and no Vydia policy was found accepting spoken-word, podcast, or audiobook audio. **Do NOT submit books, audiobooks, or podcast episodes to Vydia.** (Full verdict in `audio-vydia-dsp-expansion-2026-11-20.md`, Section 1.)

### 1B. The podcast and the audiobooks carry music beds — and each bed is a registered composition

Every podcast episode and every audiobook carries intro/outro music and chapter-transition beds. Each bed is a new composition:

- Composed by Chais Kenyatta Hill, registered with ASCAP before release, publisher XLVIIIBlock LLC, 100%.
- Released as its own Vydia single (WAV stereo 44.1 kHz/16-bit, square sRGB JPEG cover 1600×1600, label XLVIIIBlocks) — the same shape as the 15 singles — so the bed earns streaming + publishing royalties on its own, apart from the episode or audiobook it sits inside.
- **Status: no music-bed files exist yet.** No beds for the podcast, no beds for the audiobooks. The lane starts when the beds are composed. Order when they are: Transmissions theme first, then Book 05's intro/outro, then the rest.
- **Vydia §2(c) flag:** the Vydia agreement §2(c) says no third-party licence or administration for Client Content during the Term without Vydia's prior written consent. If a Vydia-distributed track is used as a bed inside a podcast episode or audiobook distributed elsewhere, check §2(c) first and get consent if it applies. Do not assume it is clear. This is a flag, not a verdict.

### 1C. The books contain lyrics — and lyrics are compositions

The dictated lyrics (Blocks 1–23, `~/workspace/music/lyrics/lyrics-2026-11-19-chais-dictated.md`) are compositions. Where a book prints or quotes lyrics, the composition travels with the text, and the publishing record rides in the book's copyright/credits pages. Practical steps per book:

- Copyright page carries the publishing line: "Compositions by Chais Kenyatta Hill, ASCAP IPI 1247873912, published by XLVIIIBlock LLC."
- Vydia Compositions is currently EMPTY — attach the lyrics per release when the singles land (open item from the 19 Nov upload set).
- Register every work with ASCAP **before** any placement airs (sync-kit rule).

### 1D. Sync licensing — the book's soundtrack placed in film, TV, and ads

The soundtrack is pitchable for sync right now, and sync is where a composition earns its biggest single cheques plus backend performance royalties (ASCAP + cue sheets). The sync lane is already in motion — see Section 3C for the live status of each platform. The book angle for sync: each book's soundtrack is pitched as a themed set (e.g. Book 03's homecoming songs for a music-documentary brief; Book 05's origin songs for a coming-up story), so a placement promotes the book and the book promotes the placement.

**What the composition angle is NOT:** it is not a way to smuggle a text book onto a music distributor, it is not a token or NFT sale of any book/chapter/audio, and it is not an earnings promise. Royalty percentages describe what a platform pays per sale or stream; they do not predict how many copies sell or how many streams land.
